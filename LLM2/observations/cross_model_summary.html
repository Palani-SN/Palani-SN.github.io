  • Orig. refused / Orig. complied — the un-ablated model's own behaviour. A low harmful figure means weak baseline refusal training, not a pipeline failure.
  • Abl. complied — harmful prompts the ablated model answered: the attack's success rate. Higher = ablation worked. On the harmless side it is a damage check — a drop below ~99 means ablation degraded ordinary helpfulness.
  • Gate escalated — prompts FastGate scored at or above gate θ and forwarded to the ExchangeClassifier. On the harmless side this is the false-alarm count.
  • C++ blocked / allowed — the two-stage Constitutional Classifiers++ verdict, the headline safety-restoration number.
  • Coords used — the 36-coordinate signature as a fraction of the full (layers+1) × d_model activation tensor the all-dims baseline scores.
  • Latencies exclude the LLM-as-Judge call in every condition. Classifier++ latency includes a full response generation on the cleared path, so it stays comparable to the other two columns.
